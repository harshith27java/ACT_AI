================================================================================
CYBERSECURITY INCIDENT REPORT — OPERATION NORTHSTAR
================================================================================
CLASSIFICATION: SYNTHETIC / TRAINING DATA
This report is explicitly fictional and was created as a demonstration dataset
for the ACT (Automated Content Transformation) project. All organizations,
indicators, and events are invented. No real intelligence is contained here.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On 12 August 2026, the fictional organization Meridian Utilities detected
unauthorized access in its corporate network. The intrusion, tracked internally
as Operation Northstar, began with a phishing campaign against finance staff
and progressed to the encryption of 34 endpoints. Customer-facing systems were
not affected. The incident was contained within 72 hours.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INCIDENT TIMELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 0 — 12 August 2026, 03:14 UTC: Initial phishing emails delivered to 118
employees, impersonating a payroll software update. Nine users opened the
attachment; three entered credentials on a fake single sign-on page.

Day 0 — 05:02 UTC: Attacker authenticated to the corporate VPN using stolen
credentials for a finance department account. Multi-factor prompts were
approved through push fatigue (14 repeated prompts).

Day 1 — 13 August: Lateral movement via Server Message Block (SMB) protocol.
Attacker staged tooling on two file servers and exfiltrated an estimated
4.2 GB of internal finance documents to an external storage service.

Day 2 — 14 August, 22:47 UTC: Ransomware payload executed across 34 endpoints
in the finance and HR departments. Files were encrypted with a .northstar
extension and ransom notes were dropped.

Day 3 — 15 August: Incident response retained. Affected hosts isolated. Domain
password reset enforced. Backups restored from immutable snapshots.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ATTACK VECTOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The investigation identified phishing emails as the initial access vector.
The lure used a cloned payroll portal. Credential theft was followed by
multi-factor push-fatigue approval, VPN access, and lateral movement over SMB.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AFFECTED SYSTEMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 34 user endpoints (Windows 11, finance and HR departments)
- 2 Windows file servers used for staging
- Corporate VPN gateway (credential abuse; no device compromise found)
- Finance document repository (4.2 GB exfiltrated)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. OBSERVED INDICATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Phishing emails from the domain payroll-updates.example (synthetic domain)
- Fake single sign-on page hosted at hr-portal-verify.example (synthetic)
- VPN sessions from IP ranges 203.0.113.0/24 (RFC 5737 documentation range)
- SMB administrative share access outside business hours
- Ransom note file: RESTORE-FILES.txt
- Encrypted file extension: .northstar

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. IMPACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 34 endpoints encrypted; 11 days of downtime for affected departments
- 4.2 GB of finance documents exfiltrated
- No evidence of impact to customer-facing or operational technology systems
- Estimated recovery cost (fictional): 180,000 USD

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. MITIGATION AND CONTAINMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Affected hosts isolated within 4 hours of detection
- Domain-wide credential reset and MFA re-enrollment enforced
- Number matching enabled for all MFA push approvals
- Immutable backups restored; recovery completed on 17 August
- Phishing domain reported to the registrar and blocked at the mail gateway

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Enforce phishing-resistant MFA (FIDO2 keys) for all remote access.
2. Deploy email authentication (DMARC with quarantine policy) — currently
   DMARC is configured in monitor-only mode.
3. Segment the finance VLAN to restrict SMB traffic between workstations.
4. Reduce VPN session lifetime and require device health checks.
5. Conduct quarterly phishing simulation exercises for finance and HR.
6. Retain endpoint detection telemetry for at least 180 days.
